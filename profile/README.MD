# Open Secure AI Alliance 

**Building a safer AI future in the open**

[Website](https://secureaialliance.org) · [Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/opensecureaialliance/shared_invite/zt-48aexc4mu-toSufFfbSxcdwcb_vVHupQ)

---

The **Open Secure AI Alliance**  builds and shares an open defensive stack of AI models, tools, and techniques to strengthen cyber defense and secure software and AI agents—giving defenders everywhere capabilities they can inspect, adapt, trust, and control.

---

## Working Groups

Our work is organized into Working Groups, each focused on a specific domain. Working Groups are proposed by any Alliance member and approved by the Steering Committee.

| Working Group | Repository | Status |
|---|---|---|
| SAFE — Shared AI Findings Exchange | [wg-safe](https://github.com/OpenSecureAIAlliance/wg-safe) | Approved |

To propose a new Working Group, see the [governance repository](https://github.com/OpenSecureAIAlliance/governance).
